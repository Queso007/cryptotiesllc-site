Privacy Policy — GTA 6 Secrets Journal

Effective: November 20, 2026
GTA 6 Secrets Journal is published by Crypto Ties LLC, Wilkes-Barre, PA, USA.

GTA 6 Secrets Journal is an unofficial fan guide and checklist for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Information the app collects: none

GTA 6 Secrets Journal does not collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising tracker in the app.

Information stored on your device

Your checklist progress (which secrets you have marked as found) and which sections you have unlocked are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them. (Purchases are also recorded by Google Play, so an unlock can be restored if you reinstall — see Payments.)

Payments (section unlocks)

Three sections — Secret Loot, Secret Treasures, and Secret Cars — are each unlocked with a one-time $2.00 purchase through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed by Google Play, not by GTA 6 Secrets Journal or Crypto Ties LLC. We do not see or store your card details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

GTA 6 Secrets Journal is a game guide for a game rated M (Mature 17+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
