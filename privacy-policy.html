Privacy Policy — TradeInvoice

Effective: November 12, 2026
TradeInvoice is published by Crypto Ties LLC, Wilkes-Barre, PA, USA.

TradeInvoice is an invoice and estimate maker for tradespeople. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Information the app collects: none

TradeInvoice does not collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising tracker in the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — your business profile and logo, your clients, and your estimates and invoices — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (for example, saving a backup file or printing / sharing an invoice as a PDF). Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

Payments (TradeInvoice Pro)

The optional TradeInvoice Pro subscription ($9.99/month, unlimited estimates and invoices) is purchased through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed by Google Play, not by TradeInvoice or Crypto Ties LLC. We do not see or store your card details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

TradeInvoice is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
